Privacy Policy

Smart Invoice Saver is a private Google Apps Script used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account.

It reads receipt and invoice emails in the owner's Gmail, saves them as PDF files in the owner's Google Drive, logs them in the owner's Google Sheet, and adds Gmail labels to processed emails.

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or sent anywhere outside the owner's Google account. The tool has no other users.

The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
